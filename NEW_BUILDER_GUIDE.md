# Guide for New Builders

## You lead the product

You decide what problem matters, who the work should help, and what the product should become. AI teams help discover, plan, build, and check the work; they do not silently take over those decisions.

## The four rooms

### Product Room

Use this room for needs, ideas, workflows, priorities, and reactions. You do not need a complete specification. Product helps separate confirmed intent from possibilities and open questions.

### Planning Room

Architecture turns an approved outcome into a small, testable implementation brief. It plans the next evidence-producing slice in detail and leaves later work lighter until current results teach us more.

### Workshop

Engineering inspects the real repository and the exact brief revision. It returns:

- **READY** when responsible implementation can begin;
- **QUESTION** when a material interpretation needs a decision;
- **BLOCKER** when a concrete conflict prevents responsible work;
- **ESCALATE** when the normal review exchange cannot resolve a material dispute.

Engineering builds READY work and reports what actually changed and what verification actually ran.

### Evaluation Room

Users, operators, stakeholders, or representative evidence tell us what happened in practice: what was useful, confusing, frustrating, risky, surprising, or apparently defective. QA can investigate defect-like results.

## The project board

**Ideas / Backlog -> Up Next -> In Progress -> Ready to Evaluate -> Accepted**

- **Ideas / Backlog** - possibilities not scheduled next.
- **Up Next** - an outcome selected for near-term work.
- **In Progress** - Engineering is implementing it.
- **Ready to Evaluate** - Architecture says the implementation matches its technical brief.
- **Accepted** - the Project Lead or responsible stakeholder has evaluated the outcome and intends to retain it.

Technical completion and product acceptance are different decisions. Correctly built work may still teach the team to revise or reject the idea.

## You do not need every answer today

`TBD` is valid. Resolve only what blocks the next useful outcome.

## The loop

**Discover -> Define -> Plan -> Review -> Build -> Accept -> Evaluate -> Improve**
