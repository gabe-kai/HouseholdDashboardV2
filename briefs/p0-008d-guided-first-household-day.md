## Why

A–C provide secure setup and management operations, but Welcome stops after account/household basics. A parent needs a short guide that uses those real records, explains the first family's work, and returns safely to unfinished choices. The complete setup, access, removal, backup, reset and restore journey must be usable through the app.

## Learning question

Can a parent reach a useful family day, correct or skip choices, resume on another device and manage/reset/restore the household without coaching or duplicate records?

## Player experience

Your account → Your household → Your people → School days → First work. Only the first two are required. The manager appears once; Add another person keeps the compact family list open. School and work have clear skip choices. Before saving work, see the family's actual names, checklists and next dates, then correct a choice without starting over. Finish shows a useful recap and Go to Today / View household. A small dismissible Finish setup checklist and Settings entry let the parent return when ready.

## Behavioral contract

1. **Required entry and five named steps.** Preserve A's owner-issued invitation, fragment exchange and first-manager authority at `/welcome`. Expand to the five Product steps with a compact phone progress indicator and quiet desktop rail. After required basics, Continue opens Your people in the saved manager destination `/household/setup`; Settings resumes that guide. Save and finish later commits a valid saved boundary and permits normal use; an incomplete dirty draft uses Keep editing / Discard before leaving, not a partial domain save. Back to an already saved Account reviews the current identity and offers existing account maintenance; it never submits another account. Household review loads saved name/timezone. Account and confirmed household basics remain the only conditions for `setupRequired`; optional incompletion cannot block normal sign-in.

2. **Guide metadata belongs to the household snapshot (D-056).** Extend A's saved progress with versioned optional choices, stable domain links, finish/dismissal state and enough nonsecret operation identity for recovery. Distinguish not yet chosen, completed and explicitly skipped. Saved boundaries survive reload, server restart and another authorized device. Unsaved ordinary form drafts can stay local; errors/conflicts retain them, while password/token drafts are never stored. The guide reads current records by ID and shows existing saved entries rather than copies. Deleted/departed/ended records are shown truthfully or omitted; a completion flag never recreates them. Reference cleanup must preserve C's unused-person deletion and D-025 unused-work deletion.

3. **Save/retry consistency.** Reuse existing domain services and their authorization/validation rather than duplicating their business rules. A guide save commits its domain change, corresponding progress/link and bound receipt in one household transaction. Use a stable mutation ID for an ambiguous retry and revalidate actor, household, installation epoch, expected progress/domain versions and preview basis inside the commit boundary. Same command returns the same result; changed payload/scope is rejected; competing changes conflict without lost records or overwritten decisions. If account creation loses its response, recover the issued session/progress or offer normal sign-in/owner recovery; never make a second manager. Do not turn guide configuration saves into checklist-outbox work.

4. **People stays quick.** Show the current manager once and other nondeparted people in saved family order. Add name + Adult/Child, save with C's Member + Approval required defaults, then keep Add another person available without traversing a detail menu for every entry. People may be complete with only the manager. Extra profiles/groups/roles/sign-in can wait. Review corrections edit the same saved person; removal uses C's preview/confirmation and safe used/unused semantics. Set up sign-in remains discoverable from saved people and recap, with copy/QR, expiry and explicit resulting identity.

5. **School is optional and real.** Configure dates/usual weekdays through the existing calendar service and offer Not now as a saved complete choice. Suggest weekdays only for confirmation; do not invent year dates. Show/edit all existing years and preserve exceptions/edition semantics. Skipping an existing configured calendar neither clears it nor changes its domain facts. An absent calendar remains unknown. Calendar conflicts retain drafts; its normal unstarted reconciliation, first-action locks and stored History behavior apply unchanged.

6. **First work is approachable.** Explain Routine: each selected person gets a checklist; Responsibility: one accountable owner per applicable day. Offer either or both, followed by another saved item if wanted, plus Finish setup for now without work. Name, a multi-select routine audience or responsibility assignment, recurrence/daypart and ordered steps are directly editable at the guide's main form level; avoid forcing Name → Edit → Save for every field or reopening a picker for each routine person. Use existing steps/ordering/applicability and secondary advanced assignment/scheduled-work controls. Examples remain explanatory text or explicitly selected drafts until Save. Returning to saved work edits that definition through its normal current/future and lock rules, not a cloned first-work record.

7. **Read-only authoritative review.** Add the missing draft-routine preview narrowly and extend/reuse responsibility draft preview. Resolve household-local dates, saved audience/eligibility, school step filtering, assignment phase and scheduled additions through the same domain evaluators as normal work. Show actual participant/final-owner names, ordered work and next applicable dates. Repeating responsibility patterns show the next seven household dates and scheduled-work/final-owner meaning. If no executable work falls in that window, show the next known applicable date beyond it or an explicit no-known-date/context reason. Never invent a date for an unconfigured school calendar or an empty/Unassigned plan. Preview creates no occurrences, execution locks, turn advancement or domain records. Return directly to the edited field/section while retaining the rest of the draft. Save must recheck the reviewed source versions and household date; a changed source/date asks for fresh review rather than silently changing the starting date.

8. **Recap and quiet completion.** Recap reads saved work/people and current next-date truth, with Go to Today and View household. Normal member sign-in reaches their own Today. When only children have assignments, manager Today stays their own and explains the empty state with a direct Household link. Distinguish future-only schedules, school-filtered emptiness and no work created. The optional Finish setup checklist is small, dismissible and reachable from Household Settings; explicit skips produce no permanent badge/warning or forced guide at login. Wall enrollment is an optional link using existing display management, not a sixth required setup step.

9. **Existing household and details.** Upgrade populated through-019 data without seeding, recreating accounts or forcing onboarding. Existing households may open the guide from Settings and see/edit actual records; do not invent historical completion times. Household details provides saved name/timezone and versioned safe correction. Require confirmation of timezone initially; once dependent dated calendar/work/activity exists, show timezone read-only with an explanation. This slice does not implement relocation of dated household history. Existing optional guide choices and dismissal are preserved when records are later edited normally.

10. **Authority and live recovery.** Full guide/progress/recap reads and writes require a current nondeparted member with `household.lifecycle.manage`; owner/setup principals retain only A's required entry/recovery authority. Each people/calendar/work/invitation command also checks its ordinary underlying grant. Customized access receives honest unavailable sections/skip/exit paths and no grant elevation. Add scoped progress invalidation and reconnect/visibility re-reads without private contents or display-guide data. Pin dirty draft baselines; incoming updates may require a new review but cannot replace dirty values silently. Fence responses and retry identities by session/household/epoch/request/version. Revocation/departure/reset/restore retires inappropriate drafts and delayed results without resurrecting setup or work.

11. **Lifecycle remains connected.** Settings groups Household details, Finish setup and Backups, with Reset household secondary and Clear activity history distinct. Reset gives fresh guide state and preserves the backup catalog/installation owner. Restore recovers the snapshot's guide choices/record links while applying B's credential sanitation and a fresh installation epoch; supported pre-D backups adopt safely without forcing required recreation. Extend staged restore sanitation to retire every C member-access claim purpose, including still-unconsumed/unexpired invitation, password-reset and restore-sign-in values. Old tokens cannot preview or redeem access after restore; a newly authorized link can. Do not restore old guide retries as new operations or reopen consumed invitations. C's privacy/removal and unused-reference rules apply to restored guide data as well.

12. **Interaction quality.** One focused phone screen, comfortable desktop form width, Back, one primary action and optional exit. Keep actions reachable above a phone keyboard; use inline errors/help, retained ordinary values, compact saved feedback, visible focus and accessible touch/keyboard ordering. Browser Back/direct reload and ordinary app navigation respect existing Keep editing / Discard behavior. Use Username, Password, Invitation and Sign-in consistently. Settings return and guide routes preserve session/sync/outbox ownership above views and display-principal precedence.
